Stamped support

Stamped is a passport for the things you do: the shows you go to, and the films, shows, books, albums, games and restaurants you get to. If you can think it, you can stamp it.

Get in touch

Questions, problems, or ideas: help@getstampedapp.com. We read everything.

Common questions

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings › Delete Account. Your account, passport, photos and connections are removed from Stamped's servers right away.
  • Report or block someone: open their profile and tap the menu. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
  • Choose who sees your passport: Settings › Privacy sets who can follow you and what your followers see.
